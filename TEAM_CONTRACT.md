# THE GROUNDING PACT: ANTI-ABANDONMENT CONTRACT
### Team Discovery Channel Gaming Expedition

We, the founding crew of **Team Discovery Channel**:
* **flaxos** (🚀)
* **Scotty** (⚡)

solemnly pledge on this date to uphold this pact so that we **actually finish and play our game** rather than abandoning another idea in the dev graveyard.

---

### Article I: The Primary Covenant
> **"The 'Graybox First' Covenant"**
We agree that all decisions bend to this rule. No asset, no refactor, and no clever idea shall bypass the requirement to keep the game playable.

### Article II: Target Timeframe
We commit to delivering Milestone 1 within **The 48-Hour Weekend Jam Sprint**. If a feature threatens this timeline, the feature is cut, not the deadline.

### Article III: The Scope Creep Defense
1. **The 'One In, One Out' Rule**: If someone suggests a new feature during development, they must nominate an existing feature of equal size to be removed from the milestone.
2. **The Fun Test**: If an idea cannot be tested with graybox cubes in under 2 hours with our coding agents, it waits for Milestone 2.
3. **No Solo Hero Rewrites**: No one rewrites the core netcode or engine loop at 2 AM without squad consent.

### Article IV: The Friday Playtest Rule
Every Friday evening, we meet, we play whatever build exists, we celebrate what works, and we log bugs. Game dev is our creative community hobby—it must stay fun!
