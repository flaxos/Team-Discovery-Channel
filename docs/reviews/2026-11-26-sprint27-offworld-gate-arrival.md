## Reusable stone platform

The `stargate_dhd` preset uses `OffworldStargate.tscn`. In another scene, place its root 3.50 m above the floor with local `+Z` toward the main approach. The base and gate rotate together. The scene inherits `Stargate.tscn` and uses its DHD binding, chevron, horizon, Kawoosh and extraction nodes. A single threshold slab spans the gate plane at 0.75 m above the room floor. The centre of the imported inner rim meets the slab to within 1 mm and the event horizon starts at slab height.

The stonework uses four wide front treads, a landing, and three rear steps. The footprint is 6.88 m wide × 6.50 m deep. A single convex collision surface follows both flights as continuous slopes. It reaches the slab height on both sides of the gate plane and participates in the existing navigation bake. The stock operative crosses the plane while grounded in either direction. The baked route also reaches the DHD around the base. ## DHD and hangar dressing

The original native DHD exterior is a chamfered stone pedestal with a bronze collar, an inclined dish, twelve decorative keycaps and a glass indicator. Its deck tilts 15° toward the authored approach. The visible envelope measures 0.98 × 1.254 × 0.972 m inside the 1.30 × 1.30 × 1.00 m solid body. The 64 mesh instances draw 2,184 triangles and share the stone platform's three 1K maps. The adapter reads the dialer and the replicated gate state for idle, dialing and open lamp brightness. The existing DHD HUD and objective-gated return logic still handle the interaction. Three recessed service cabinets sit in the east wall's cosmetic layer, within its existing envelope. The neutral comparison verifies identical collision, navigation, sockets and markers.
