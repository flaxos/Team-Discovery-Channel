## Questions

Answer each in a sentence. Name the room where it matters.

1. **Tells.** Could you read the **?**, **!** and **!!** markers in time from the third-person camera?
2. **Fairness of sight.** Were you ever seen when you thought you were hidden, or missed when you thought you were exposed?
3. **Hearing.** Did unsuppressed fire draw sentries from about the distance you expected? Was the suppressed MP5 quiet enough to be worth choosing?
4. **Alarm runner.** When a Jaffa ran for the alarm, did you notice in time, and could you stop him?
5. **Consequences.** After the alarm, did the klaxon, searching sentries and reinforcements feel like pressure you could still play through, or like a wall?
6. **Cover.** Did the Jaffa at the checkpoint and the upper antechamber read as fighting from cover? Could you flank them out of it?
7. **Routes.** Was the quiet route worth its length? Was the checkpoint worth avoiding?
8. **Replay.** Would you play it again by the other route? Why or why not?
